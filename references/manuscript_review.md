# Manuscript review and integration

Use for a manuscript review, revision acceptance, submission preparation, or coordinated result replacement. A local edit needs only checks for its effects. Scientific interpretation belongs in [scientific evidence](scientific_rigor.md), mathematical changes in [mathematical methods](mathematical-methods.md), and connected language in [prose conventions](prose_style.md).

## Identity, scope, and state

Establish the active root document, bibliography, relevant assets, build route, and working revision from current content. Inspect existing changes and version ancestry when a collaborator's update or similarly named manuscript makes identity uncertain. Do not infer the target from a filename or repeat an old completion report.

Distinguish inspection, language repair, structural revision, scientific review, layout, and revision acceptance. Preserve protected text and project settings from the request or an existing context record; do not require a new administrative file. Complete necessary dependent repairs, such as references after a figure-to-table conversion, within the authorised change.

For tracked edits, read both the displayed review state and the intended accepted version. Deletions can remove premises, subjects, definitions, or antecedents. Accept only the requested markup layer; preserve ordinary emphasis, mathematical notation, and scientific colours even when they resemble revision colours. A marked table row remains displayed until actually removed.

## Argument and evidence

For substantial structural work, identify each section's question and what it prepares the reader to understand next. Check whether the opening, headings, and figures make the question, contribution, primary evidence, and finding easy to locate. This is a qualitative orientation check unless reader testing is performed.

Choose the progression for the genre. Theory may follow assumptions, construction, theorem, and implication; empirical work may follow hypothesis, design, observations, and interpretation; methods work may connect problem, representation, algorithm, and evaluation. A repeated motivation must add a new implication. Rhetorical patterns diagnose gaps rather than mandate particular opening sentences.

Keep a result's target, comparator, population, conditions, aggregation, uncertainty, and source recoverable. When several experiments are present, use an overview only when it helps distinguish their questions, changed factors, and controls. Retain important adverse results and trade-offs beside the supported advantages.

When shortening, classify material by its function: central argument, supporting evidence, diagnostic detail, or duplication. Move material only when permitted and retain validity-critical qualifications with surviving claims. A removed comparator cannot support a surviving superiority claim. Explain numerical evidence in the form that best serves interpretation; do not create a table solely to duplicate a prose number.

## Tables and changing representations

Identify metric, units, improvement direction, population, and interval convention. Distinguish absolute scores, percentage changes, and score points. State what missing values mean without inventing data. Define the comparison set and emphasis rule, then update bolding and “best” claims when displayed rows change.

Choose a table when exact values dominate, and a plot when shape, ordering, density, or interval geometry matters. After an authorised conversion, preserve estimates and uncertainty, update captions and object references, and remove language describing marks that no longer exist. Inspect the affected layout separately from checking the values.

## Figures

Use [figure synthesis](../figure_synthesis_guide.md) when selecting or creating a figure and [LaTeX editing](latex_manuscript_editing.md) for inclusion. Check labels, units, scales, category meanings, comparison direction, and caption agreement. Distinguish the displayed example's domain or subset from the population used to compute an accompanying metric.

Review scientific fidelity and visual clarity independently. Inspect source exports and, when integration is in scope, their actual document placement for reading order, glyph collisions, colour contrast, line weights, caption association, and effective text size. Preserve aspect ratio and required caption spacing. Place interpretation close enough to its figure that the reader can connect them; do not impose a fixed maximum footprint or a universal paragraph-after-float rule.

Mathematical or conceptual diagrams need traceable correspondences and valid illustrated properties. They explain a construction; empirical performance requires separate evidence. An available `graph-plotting` skill supplies deeper representation and schematic guidance without being a prerequisite for manuscript editing.

## References, names, and result replacement

Keep citations attached unambiguously to their supporting claims. Authorised citation grouping is acceptable when attribution survives. For bibliography work, use [bibliography requirements](bibliography.md), including metadata and rendered-link verification at the requested depth. Inspect macros that could produce duplicate semantic prefixes in cross-references.

For a publication-name change, update visible names across in-scope text, tables, captions, and figures and preserve a mapping to immutable code, run, and result identifiers. A new label does not authorise rewriting experiment history or changing the described architecture.

Before replacing a result, identify its canonical source, comparison, population, settings, and aggregation. Regenerate requested assets reproducibly and reconcile dependent in-scope text, tables, captions, and conclusions. Search affected sources for superseded values and terms. Preserve supplied asset organisation unless a move is requested or needed; local previews do not become accepted manuscript assets automatically.

## Verification and submission

For a requested full audit, report inspected dimensions with evidence: claim support, data/definition integrity, mathematical or statistical validity, reproducibility, prose, references, and rendered integration. Use only dimensions applicable to the paper. Give each finding a location, affected claim, evidence, and concrete repair. An unresolved check limits that completion claim, not independent work.

Verify current venue requirements for submission work, including the applicable page limit, front matter, availability statements, disclosures, figure formats, bibliography, and supplement links. [TCCML guidance](tccml-neurips.md) applies only to that workshop. Use [front matter](front_matter.md) to verify the abstract's 2,000-character limit, including spaces, and any stricter venue constraint.

After edits, run affected checks and finish with a continuous contextual read. Distinguish planned, executed, verified, incorporated, and conclusive experiments: completion can leave a concern open. Report source inspection, compilation, visual review, numerical checks, experiment execution, saving, and publication as separate actions. Repeat successful checks only after a further edit, failure, or concrete unresolved concern.
