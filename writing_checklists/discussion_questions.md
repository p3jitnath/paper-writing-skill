# Discussion Checklist

Select questions relevant to the requested change and research genre. Weather/climate items apply only to those claims; this is not a required full audit for a local edit.

- Does the section synthesize rather than repeat results?
- Is the Discussion divided into distinct subsections with neutral, descriptive names suited to the argument?
- Does it explain supported strengths and contributions while giving honest, constructive criticism of material limitations and adverse findings?
- Are clarifications, nuances and criticisms phrased diplomatically, with their scientific meaning and necessary corrections preserved?
- Are the Conclusion and descriptions of this or other papers written in past tense, retaining the distinction between completed and proposed work?
- Does it build on local interpretations already given with the results rather than deferring all explanation?
- Is each proposed mechanism supported and are alternatives considered?
- Are forced response, internal variability, model, observational, and scenario uncertainty separated where relevant?
- Are interpolation, temporal transfer, spatial transfer, and forcing extrapolation distinguished?
- Are limitations tied to the conclusions or uses they restrict?
- Are general limitations and scope qualifications neutral and concentrated in Discussion or Scope, with validity-critical conditions retained beside their results?
- Is societal or operational value supported by decision-relevant evidence?
- Does the final paragraph name the next decisive test rather than a generic future-work list?
- Does the final sentence of the Conclusion, or the concluding Discussion, end on the strongest evidence-supported implication in concise and impactful language without introducing a new claim or exceeding the paper's scope?
