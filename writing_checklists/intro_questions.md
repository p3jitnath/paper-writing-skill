# Introduction Checklist

Select questions relevant to the requested change and research genre. Weather/climate items apply only to those claims; this is not a required full audit for a local edit.

- Are the first two prose paragraphs reserved for scientific background, context, and motivation, with the explicit gap, proposed method, contributions, and results deferred until paragraph three or later?
- Does the complete introduction cite at least five and at most ten distinct verified sources? Count unique works mechanically in the intended accepted text: expand grouped citations, deduplicate repeated sources and duplicate records, and exclude comments, deleted review text, and uncited bibliography entries.
- Does each citation support its nearby claim, including any background claims in the opening two paragraphs, without references added merely to reach the minimum?
- Does the opening identify a specific Earth-system problem, scale, and consequence?
- Is the gap scientific, methodological, computational, or operational rather than generic “low accuracy”?
- Does the core insight precede the model name?
- Are contributions written as findings with evidence boundaries?
- Does each contribution map to a result or diagnostic?
- Does the preview name the reference, metric, domain, period/lead time, and uncertainty needed to interpret the headline result?
- Are physical, causal, generalization, and operational claims no stronger than the evidence?
